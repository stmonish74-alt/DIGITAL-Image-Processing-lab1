How the methods work

Region filling

A flood-fill operation starts from background pixels on the image border. The unvisited background regions are enclosed holes. Those regions are changed to foreground, while the outside background stays unchanged.

Morphological closing

Closing is dilation followed by erosion using a structuring element. It can close small gaps and narrow openings, but it may not fill large enclosed holes. Compare its output with region filling.

How line detection works

Directional masks

A small convolution mask responds strongly to line-like structures aligned with its orientation. Four masks are used to examine horizontal, vertical and both diagonal directions.

Response comparison

The directional responses are thresholded and combined. The final output overlays detected line structures on the original road image. Other edges and road textures may also respond, so the result is a line-structure demonstration rather than a full autonomous lane tracker.

About this model

This academic demonstration trains SVM, Random Forest and K-Nearest Neighbors classifiers using scikit-learn’s Optical Recognition of Handwritten Digits dataset. It is not an MNIST model, and predictions can vary with handwriting style, image quality and preprocessing.
